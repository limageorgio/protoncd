Safety Training Programs
We Offer

Comprehensive regulatory safety training programs compliant with Brazilian NR standards. All courses delivered by a CREA-registered instructor with official certification.

Training Programs We Offer:

  • NR-10 — Electrical Safety — Basic course (40h) + Supplementary/SEP course (40h). Electrical hazard awareness, lockout/tagout, arc flash protection, emergency procedures
  • NR-12 — Machinery Safety — Safe operation of industrial equipment, machine guarding, risk zones, emergency stops, maintenance procedures (8–16h)
  • NR-33 — Confined Spaces — Worker level (16h), Supervisor level (40h), and Rescuer level. Atmospheric monitoring, entry procedures, emergency rescue
  • NR-35 — Working at Heights — Fall protection, anchor points, PPE selection, rescue planning for work above 2 meters (8h)
  • NR-05 — CIPA (Safety Committee) — Internal Accident Prevention Committee member training, accident investigation, risk mapping (20h)
  • NR-06 — PPE Usage — Correct selection, use, maintenance, and storage of personal protective equipment (4–8h)
  • First Aid — Emergency response, CPR, AED usage, hemorrhage control, fracture immobilization, burn treatment

Legal Requirement

  • Training is legally mandatory per MTE (Ministry of Labor). Workers cannot perform hazardous activities without valid training certificates
  • Companies without training records face administrative fines starting at R$2,000 per infraction and equipment embargo
  • CREA-registered instructor required for engineering-related courses (NR-10, NR-12, NR-33, NR-35)

Training Programs

NR-10 Electrical Safety

Basic course (40h) and Supplementary/SEP course (40h). Electrical hazard identification, safe work practices, lockout/tagout, arc flash protection, emergency procedures, and authorization of qualified workers.

NR-12 Machinery Safety

Safe operation of industrial machinery and equipment (8–16h). Machine guarding principles, risk zones, emergency stop systems, maintenance lockout procedures, and operator qualification per NR-12.

NR-33 Confined Spaces

Worker (16h), Supervisor (40h), and Rescuer levels. Confined space identification, atmospheric monitoring, entry permits, ventilation procedures, rescue planning, and emergency response protocols.

NR-35 Working at Heights

Fall protection training (8h) for work above 2 meters. Anchor point selection, harness inspection, access equipment, rescue procedures, and medical fitness requirements per NR-35.

NR-05 CIPA Training

Internal Accident Prevention Committee member training (20h). Risk mapping, accident investigation, workplace inspection techniques, hazard identification, and committee governance per NR-05.

Custom Safety Programs

Tailored safety training programs designed for your specific operations. PPE usage (NR-06), first aid, fire brigade formation, ergonomics (NR-17), hazardous materials handling, and emergency action plans.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Yes. NR training is legally mandatory under Brazilian labor law. The Ministry of Labor (MTE) requires that all workers exposed to specific hazards receive proper training before performing their activities. NR-10 (electrical), NR-12 (machinery), NR-33 (confined spaces), and NR-35 (work at heights) all mandate formal training with specific curricula and minimum hours. Companies that fail to train their workers face administrative fines starting at R$2,000 per infraction, equipment embargo, work stoppages, and criminal liability in case of accidents. Training records must be documented and available for MTE inspection at all times.

Upon successful completion, each participant receives an official training certificate with legal validity in Brazil. The certificate includes: participant name, CPF (Brazilian ID), training program name and NR reference, workload (hours), date and location, instructor name, CREA registration number, and course content summary. For engineering-related courses (NR-10, NR-12, NR-33, NR-35), the certificate is backed by a CREA-registered instructor, which is a legal requirement. All certificates are issued in Portuguese (as required by MTE) with an English translation available upon request.

Yes. We offer bilingual training sessions (English and Portuguese) specifically designed for foreign companies operating in Brazil. The instructor delivers the content in English while providing all official documentation, certificates, and regulatory references in Portuguese as required by Brazilian authorities. This ensures your international workforce fully understands the safety procedures while maintaining full legal compliance. Bilingual training is available for all NR programs including NR-10, NR-12, NR-33, and NR-35.

Course duration varies by NR and training level: NR-10 Basic is 40 hours, NR-10 Supplementary (SEP) is 40 hours, NR-12 varies from 8 to 16 hours depending on machinery complexity, NR-33 Worker is 16 hours, NR-33 Supervisor is 40 hours, NR-35 Basic is 8 hours, NR-05 CIPA is 20 hours, and NR-06 PPE is typically 4–8 hours. All courses include both theoretical instruction and practical exercises. Training can be delivered in consecutive days or spread across multiple sessions to accommodate your operational schedule.

Most NR training certifications are valid for 2 years and require periodic recycling (refresher training). NR-10 requires recycling every 2 years or whenever there is a change in procedures, equipment, or work conditions. NR-12 recycling depends on new equipment or procedure changes. NR-33 requires recycling every year for workers and every 2 years for supervisors. NR-35 requires recycling every 2 years. We maintain a training calendar for our clients, sending advance notifications before certifications expire to ensure continuous compliance and avoid gaps that could result in MTE fines.
