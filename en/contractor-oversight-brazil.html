Most project failures are not caused by a single technical error. They accumulate through small, undocumented concessions that nobody owns. Independent construction supervision and third-party management in Brazil exist to break that pattern before it reaches acceptance.

Oversight Lifecycle

From Qualification
to Verified Closure

Five stages, each with a defined output. The model works for a single supplier or a multi-contractor capital project.

01 · Qualification

Scope capability, technical references, CREA registration, ART history, insurance, quality system and safety indicators. Output: a documented qualification opinion and risk classification.

02 · Mobilization

Kick-off alignment, responsibility matrix, document control, inspection and test plan review, access and safety onboarding. Output: an approved mobilization checklist.

03 · Execution

Risk-based field presence, sample verification, witness of critical tests, progress validation, interface coordination and weekly reporting. Output: verified progress and live NCR register.

04 · Verification

Corrective-action evidence review, retest or re-inspection, acceptance or rejection, residual-risk assessment. Output: closure decision with objective evidence.

05 · Closure

As-built and test-record validation, punch-list closure, lessons learned and supplier performance scorecard. Output: a handover package that supports warranty and maintenance.

NCR Workflow

Every Finding
Has a Path to Closure

One register, six states and a single rule: a non-conformance is closed only when the evidence proves the correction, not when someone says it is done.

Finding class Definition Notification Acceptance impact
Critical Imminent safety, legal non-compliance or asset integrity risk. Immediate, verbal and written, to contractor and owner leadership. Blocks the affected activity and the related acceptance milestone.
Major Performance, durability or contractual requirement not met. Within 1 business day, with a corrective-action plan required. Blocks acceptance of the specific system until verified correction.
Minor Deviation without immediate safety or performance impact. Recorded in the weekly report with an agreed due date. May be accepted with a documented punch-list commitment.
Observation Improvement opportunity or early trend signal. Logged for trend analysis and preventive action. No acceptance impact; feeds lessons learned.

Service levels that create momentum

Targets are agreed at kick-off: acknowledgement of new demands within 1 business day, ordinary technical responses within 2 business days, complex analyses within 5 business days, and immediate notification for critical safety findings. Actual performance is reported monthly — including our own.

Evidence Standard

If It Is Not Recorded,
It Did Not Happen

Each finding must survive a simple test: could a third party, months later, understand what was observed, against which requirement, and whether it was corrected?

  • Time-stamped, geotagged photographs — wide, medium and detail views with a reference for scale and location.
  • Measurement records — instrument identification, calibration validity, reading, unit and measurement conditions.
  • Normative reference — the exact standard, clause, drawing, specification or contract item that establishes the requirement.
  • Signed field records — daily logs, witness sheets and test certificates signed by the parties present.
  • Chain of custody — for samples, failed components or materials subject to laboratory analysis.
  • Closure evidence — before-and-after records, retest results and a clear verification statement.
  • Indexed data room — every record linked to an NCR ID, asset tag, location and responsible party.
Traceability by design

Each NCR connects requirement → observation → evidence → corrective action → verification → closure. The chain is auditable without asking the site team to reconstruct history.

Visual evidence

Photographic records with context, scale and location — not isolated close-ups without reference.

Measured evidence

Instrumented readings with calibrated equipment and documented measurement conditions.

Vanity metrics do not survive an audit. These indicators are defined, sourced and reviewed with the owner every month.

Safety Interface

Safety Findings
Follow a Faster Clock

Brazilian NRs create specific duties for contractors and owners. Our role is to observe, notify, register and escalate — never to replace the contractor's legal safety responsibility.

  • NR-06 — PPE selection, issuance, training and records.
  • NR-10 — electrical safety, authorization and energized-work controls.
  • NR-18 — construction site safety, access, lifting and temporary installations.
  • NR-35 — work at heights, fall protection and rescue planning.
  • APR / PT — technical support for risk analysis and permit-to-work interfaces with the owner's safety team.
  • Severe risk — immediate communication to contractor management and owner safety leadership, with written record and escalation.

No command of contractor crews

Proton advises, verifies and escalates. The contractor remains responsible for directing its workers, enforcing safety rules, supplying PPE and responding to emergencies. Proton may withdraw its own team from an unsafe area and document the decision.

Preventive presence

Field observation focused on leading indicators, not only on accidents.

Immediate notification

Critical conditions communicated at once, followed by a written record.

Documented escalation

Refusal, omission or continuity of unsafe work recorded with times and recipients.

Workplace Safety & NR Compliance
Scope Limits

Clear Boundaries.
Clean Accountability.

An advisor who promises everything becomes a liability. These limits are disclosed before work starts and written into the governance charter.

We do not execute

No installation, correction, retubing, rework, commissioning maneuver or physical intervention.

We do not supply

No equipment, materials, spare parts or test loads. This keeps the acceptance opinion independent.

We do not direct crews

Contractor workers remain under contractor management. We do not issue operational orders or discipline personnel.

We do not replace QA/QC or SESMT

Contractor quality and safety teams remain in place and accountable. We provide independent verification.

We are not legal representatives

No representation before labor, tax, environmental, fire or municipal authorities; no signing of administrative defenses.

We do not certify the unseen

Findings reflect the conditions and access available on the inspection date. Hidden defects outside the agreed scope are not warranted.
