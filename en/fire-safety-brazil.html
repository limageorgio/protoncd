Fire Protection Requirements
in Brazil

Fire safety is enforced at the state level in Brazil. Every commercial, industrial, and multi-family residential building must maintain compliant fire protection systems to obtain and renew the AVCB/CLCB fire permit.

What We Inspect:

  • Sprinkler systems — Wet, dry, pre-action, and deluge systems per ABNT NBR 10897
  • Fire hydrants & standpipes — Hose stations, cabinets, pressure testing per NBR 13714
  • Fire pumps — Electric and diesel fire pumps, jockey pumps, controllers per NBR 16400
  • Fire alarms — Detection, alarm panels, manual call points, notification per NBR 17240
  • Emergency lighting — Autonomous and centralized emergency lighting, exit signage
  • Fire doors — Fire-rated assemblies, closers, seals, labeling verification
  • Fire extinguishers — Type, placement, maintenance, hydrostatic testing
  • Smoke control — Mechanical exhaust, stairwell pressurization, smoke barriers

Legal Framework

  • State Fire Department regulations — CBMGO, CBMSP, CBMMG and others issue Instruções Técnicas (ITs)
  • ABNT NBR 13714 — Fire hydrants and mangotinhos (≈ NFPA 14)
  • ABNT NBR 10897 — Automatic sprinkler systems (≈ NFPA 13)
  • ABNT NBR 17240 — Fire detection and alarm (≈ NFPA 72)

Why It's Critical:

Fire incidents are preventable

Most fire casualties result from non-functional fire protection systems — sprinklers that don't activate, alarms that don't sound, emergency exits that are blocked. Regular inspection is the difference between a controlled incident and a catastrophe.

  • Life safety — Protect building occupants with functional fire protection
  • AVCB/CLCB compliance — Required fire permit for building operation
  • Insurance requirements — Valid fire inspection for coverage and claims
  • Legal liability — Building owners bear criminal responsibility for fire safety
  • Business continuity — Fire can shut down operations permanently
  • Property protection — Functional systems limit fire damage and loss
  • Due diligence — ART-backed reports document responsible management
  • Tenant requirements — Multinational tenants demand fire code compliance
Scope

Fire Protection Systems
We Inspect

We cover all fire protection and life safety systems required by Brazilian fire codes and international standards.

Sprinkler Systems

Wet pipe, dry pipe, pre-action, and deluge systems. Valve inspection, flow testing, obstruction investigation, and head condition assessment per ABNT NBR 10897.

Fire Hydrants & Standpipes

Indoor and outdoor hydrants, hose stations, mangotinhos, standpipe systems. Pressure testing, valve operation, hose condition, and cabinet accessibility per ABNT NBR 13714.

Fire Pump Systems

Electric and diesel fire pumps, jockey pumps, controllers, and transfer switches. Flow and pressure testing, weekly/monthly run verification per ABNT NBR 16400.

Detection & Alarm

Smoke and heat detectors, alarm control panels, manual call points, audible and visual notification appliances. System programming and zone verification per ABNT NBR 17240.

Emergency Lighting & Exit Signs

Autonomous and centralized emergency lighting units, illuminated exit signs, photoluminescent signage. Battery testing, illumination levels, and pathway coverage.

Smoke Control & Pressurization

Stairwell pressurization systems, mechanical smoke exhaust, smoke barriers, and smoke dampers. Pressure differential testing and airflow verification.

Additional Systems

Fire doors and fire-rated assemblies, fire extinguishers (ABC, CO₂, water, foam), kitchen fire suppression (hood systems), gas detection and shutoff, emergency generators for fire systems, and fire department access/connections (Siamese connections).

AVCB (Auto de Vistoria do Corpo de Bombeiros) and CLCB (Certificado de Licenciamento do Corpo de Bombeiros) are fire safety permits issued by the Brazilian Fire Department. AVCB is required for commercial, industrial, and high-occupancy buildings, while CLCB applies to smaller establishments. Both certify that a building meets all fire safety requirements — including fire protection systems, emergency exits, signage, and structural fire resistance. Operating without a valid AVCB/CLCB can result in fines, building closure, and criminal liability in case of fire incidents.

Yes. Brazilian fire safety regulations are enforced at the state level by each state's Fire Department (Corpo de Bombeiros Militar). Every commercial, industrial, and multi-family residential building must obtain and maintain a valid AVCB or CLCB. This requires periodic inspection of all fire protection systems — sprinklers, hydrants, fire alarms, emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, fire doors, and smoke control. Non-compliance can result in fines, building shutdown, denial of operating permits, and criminal liability.

We inspect all fire protection systems: automatic sprinkler systems (wet, dry, pre-action, deluge), fire hydrants and standpipes, fire pump systems (electric and diesel), fire detection and alarm systems, emergency lighting and exit signage, smoke control and stairwell pressurization, fire doors, fire extinguishers, and kitchen suppression systems. All inspections follow applicable ABNT standards and state-specific Instruções Técnicas (ITs).

Brazilian fire protection standards are largely based on or equivalent to NFPA codes: ABNT NBR 10897 (sprinklers) corresponds to NFPA 13, ABNT NBR 13714 (hydrants/standpipes) to NFPA 14, ABNT NBR 17240 (fire alarm) to NFPA 72, and ABNT NBR 16400 (fire pumps) to NFPA 20. Each Brazilian state also issues its own Instruções Técnicas (ITs) that supplement the national ABNT standards. Our reports can cross-reference both Brazilian and NFPA standards for multinational clients.
