Brazilian Legal Context

  • CPC (Código de Processo Civil) — Art. 465–480 govern court-appointed expert witnesses and party technical assistants in Brazilian civil procedure
  • Perito vs. Assistente Técnico — The perito is court-appointed and impartial; the assistente técnico is party-appointed and advocates for the client's technical position
  • ART requirement — All forensic engineering reports in Brazil must be backed by an ART (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica) registered with CREA
  • CONFEA Resolution 345/1990 — Regulates engineering expert activities and professional qualification requirements

The judge selects the perito to conduct an impartial technical investigation and produce a forensic report (laudo pericial). An assistente técnico is a party-appointed technical assistant — each side in the dispute can appoint its own assistente técnico to accompany the court expert's investigation, review the laudo pericial, and submit a parecer técnico (technical opinion) defending their client's position. Both roles require a CREA-licensed engineer with an ART-registered report.
